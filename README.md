Salon Booking and Management System

About the Project

The Salon Booking and Management System is an application designed to improve salon operations by simplifying appointment scheduling, reducing customer waiting times, optimizing staff workload distribution, and improving overall customer experience.

Problem Statement

Traditional salon operations often face challenges such as:

- Unequal distribution of workload among barbers.
- Long waiting times for customers.
- Loss of potential clients due to inefficient booking.
- Poor optimization of salon resources.
- Reduced revenue due to operational inefficiencies.

Project Objectives

- Simplify the appointment booking process.
- Reduce customer waiting times.
- Distribute workloads more efficiently.
- Improve salon management and organization.
- Help increase customer satisfaction and revenue.

Proposed Solution

We propose a digital platform that allows customers to book appointments while helping salon administrators manage schedules, staff availability, and customer appointments more efficiently.
