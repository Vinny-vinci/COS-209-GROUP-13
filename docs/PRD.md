PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)

Project Title: Barbershop Booking and Workforce Optimisation System

1. Product Overview

The Barbershop Booking and Workforce Optimisation System is a proposed application designed to improve the organisation and management of barbershop operations through an appointment-booking system.

The application will allow customers to book appointments, select their preferred barbers, view available time slots, and manage existing bookings. It will also help barbershops organise their schedules and distribute appointments more effectively among available barbers.

The overall aim is to improve service delivery, reduce customer waiting times, optimise the use of available working hours, and contribute to increased business revenue.

2. Problem Statement

Many barbershops experience operational challenges that affect both customer satisfaction and business productivity. The major problems identified include:

- Unequal Workload Distribution: Some barbers may receive more appointments than others, resulting in an imbalance in workload.
- Loss of Customers: Customers may choose other barbershops when they experience poor service organisation or excessive waiting times.
- Long Waiting Times: Customers often have to wait for extended periods because appointments and service schedules are not properly organised.
- Poor Operational Efficiency: Available barbers and their working hours are not always utilised effectively.
- Reduced Revenue: Inefficient scheduling and customer loss can negatively affect the barbershop's overall income.

3. Existing Solution and Its Limitations

One proposed approach to addressing the workload and waiting-time problems is increasing the number of barbers employed by the salon.

However, increasing the workforce does not necessarily solve the underlying problem of poor scheduling and unequal workload distribution.

It may also increase operational expenses without guaranteeing improved productivity or customer satisfaction.

Therefore, a more organised approach to appointment scheduling and workforce utilisation is required.

4. Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is to develop a barbershop booking application that improves the organisation of appointments and the utilisation of available barbers' working hours.

Through the application, customers will be able to schedule appointments based on available time slots and choose their preferred barbers.

Customers who do not have a preferred barber, or whose preferred barber is unavailable, will also have the option of automatic barber assignment.

The system will consider both barber availability and existing workload when assigning appointments automatically.

This approach is intended to improve workload distribution, minimise scheduling conflicts, and provide customers with a more organised booking experience.

5. Project Objectives

The project aims to:

1. Reduce customer waiting times through organised appointment scheduling.
2. Improve workload distribution among available barbers.
3. Minimise the loss of customers resulting from poor service organisation.
4. Optimise the utilisation of barbers' available working hours.
5. Improve the overall efficiency of barbershop operations.
6. Enhance customer convenience and satisfaction.
7. Contribute to increased business revenue.

6. Target Users

The application is intended for the following categories of users:

6.1 Customers

Customers will use the application to:

- View available appointment time slots.
- Select their preferred barber.
- Choose automatic barber assignment when preferred.
- Book appointments.
- View their existing appointments.
- Cancel or reschedule appointments.

6.2 Barbers

Barbers are the service providers whose working schedules and appointments will be organised through the system.

Each barber will have an individual work schedule that determines their availability for customer bookings.

7. Functional Requirements

The application should provide the following functionalities;

7.1 Appointment Booking
- Customers should be able to book appointments through the application.
- Customers should be able to select their preferred date and available time slot.
- The system should prevent conflicting bookings.

7.2 Barber Selection

- Customers should be able to choose a specific barber.
- Customers should have the option of automatic barber assignment.
- Automatic assignment should consider barber availability and existing workload.

7.3 Availability Management

- Customers should be able to view available booking slots.
- Each barber should have an individual work schedule.
- The system should reflect changes in availability when appointments are booked, cancelled, or rescheduled.

- 7.4 Appointment Management

- Customers should be able to view their existing appointments.
- Customers should be able to cancel appointments.
- Customers should be able to reschedule appointments.
- The system should update the relevant barber's schedule following changes to an appointment.

7.5 Workload Distribution

- The system should consider the number of existing appointments assigned to each available barber.
- Automatic assignment should aim to distribute appointments fairly.
- The system should avoid assigning appointments to unavailable barbers.

8. Non-Functional Requirements

The application should satisfy the following requirements:

The application should provide the following functionalities:

8.1 Usability

The interface should be simple, clear, and easy for customers to navigate.

8.2 Reliability

The system should maintain accurate appointment records and availability information.

8.3 Performance

The application should process booking requests and display available time slots within a reasonable time.

8.4 Security

Customer information and appointment records should be protected against unauthorised access.

8.5 Consistency

Changes made to appointments should be reflected accurately in the relevant schedules.

9. Expected Benefits

The successful implementation of the application is expected to provide the following benefits:

- Reduced customer waiting times.
- Better distribution of work among barbers.
- Improved utilisation of available working hours.
- Fewer scheduling conflicts.
- Improved customer experience.
- Reduced customer loss.
- Increased operational efficiency.
- Potential improvement in business revenue.

- 10. Project Scope

The initial scope of the project focuses on developing an appointment-booking system that supports barber selection, automatic barber assignment, appointment management, availability viewing, and workload distribution.

The project will concentrate on improving scheduling and the organisation of existing barbers rather than relying on increasing the number of employees.

Additional features may be considered in future versions of the application.

11. Acceptance Criteria

The application will be considered to meet its primary requirements when:

1. Customers can successfully book appointments.
2. Customers can view available time slots.
3. Customers can select their preferred barbers.
4. Customers can choose automatic barber assignment.
5. Automatic assignments consider barber availability and workload.
6. The system prevents conflicting appointments.
7. Customers can cancel and reschedule appointments.
8. Appointment changes are reflected in barber schedules.

12. Conclusion

The Barbershop Booking and Workforce Optimisation System is intended to address common scheduling and operational challenges within barbershops.

By introducing an organised appointment-booking process and a workload-conscious barber assignment system, the application aims to improve customer experience, make better use of available working hours, and support more efficient business operations
